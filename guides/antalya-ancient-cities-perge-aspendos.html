Perge, Aspendos and Side from Antalya: where to stay and what to see

Perge was the home of Apollonius, "the Great Geometer", who gave us the words ellipse, parabola and hyperbola. A short drive away, the theatre of Aspendos is one of the best preserved of the ancient world. Antalya makes an easy base for both.

In short
  • Antalya, especially the old town of Kaleiçi, is about 20 minutes from Perge and 45 minutes from Aspendos.
  • Side, about an hour to an hour and a half east, has its own ruins and the Temple of Apollo by the sea.
  • Visit the Antalya Museum to see the statues found at Perge.

Antalya: the base for Perge and Aspendos

Stay in or near Kaleiçi, the walled old town above the harbour. In the morning drive to Perge for its colonnaded street, stadium and city gates, then on to Aspendos. Its theatre was built in the 2nd century AD by the architect Zeno and is still used for performances.

The geometer of Perge

Apollonius was born in Perge around 262 BC and worked in Alexandria. In his Conics he studied the curves made by cutting a cone, and named them. Of its eight books, four survive in Greek and three only in Arabic translation.
