A philosophy trip through Türkiye: places, routes and guides

Many of the first philosophers lived on the coasts of what is now Türkiye. You can still walk through their cities: the theatres where people gathered, the libraries, the harbours. These guides help you plan the trip, choose where to stay and know what you are looking at.

In short
  • Eighteen philosophy and art stops from Göbekli Tepe to Rumi, on one interactive map.
  • Nine ready-made routes, from the Aegean coast to Istanbul, Cappadocia, Antalya and Mesopotamia.
  • Hotel and tour suggestions for every night of every route.

The nine routes

Aegean and Pamukkale · 4 days

Ephesus, Miletus, Aphrodisias and Hierapolis: from Heraclitus to Epictetus. Nights in Selçuk, Didim and Pamukkale.

Northern Aegean · 3 days

Clazomenae near Urla, Pergamon and Assos: the shores of Anaxagoras, Galen and Aristotle. Nights in Urla, Bergama and Behramkale.

Konya and the Mediterranean · 2 days

The Mevlana Museum and Seljuk tilework in Konya, then Tarsus and Soli, home of the Stoic Chrysippus. Nights in Konya and Mersin.

Diogenes' Sinop · 2 days

The Black Sea town where Diogenes the Cynic was born. Two nights in Sinop.

Istanbul and İznik · 3 days

Hagia Sophia, the Archaeology Museums and Chora, then İznik, birthplace of Hipparchus. Two nights in Istanbul, one in İznik.

Cappadocia and Konya · 3 days

The rock churches of Göreme and the land of the Cappadocian Fathers, then Rumi's Konya. Two nights in Göreme, one in Konya.

The ancient cities of Antalya · 3 days

Perge, home of Apollonius, the theatre of Aspendos and Side. Two nights in Antalya, one in Side.

Mesopotamia: Zeugma, Göbekli Tepe, Mardin · 4 days

The Zeugma mosaics, Göbekli Tepe, one of the earliest known monumental sites, Harran and the Syriac monasteries of Mardin. Nights in Gaziantep, Şanlıurfa and Mardin.

Miletus and Bodrum · 2 days

From the first philosophers at Miletus to Herodotus and the Mausoleum at Halicarnassus. Two nights in Bodrum.
