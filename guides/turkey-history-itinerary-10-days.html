Turkey itinerary for 10 days: Istanbul, Cappadocia, Ephesus and Pamukkale

Ten days is enough for the classic triangle of Istanbul, Cappadocia and the Aegean coast, if you fly the long legs. This Turkey itinerary for 10 days is built for people who want history and ideas, with one main place each day and time to look.

In short
  • Days 1 to 3 Istanbul. Fly to Cappadocia.
  • Days 4 to 5 Cappadocia. Fly to İzmir.
  • Days 6 to 8 Ephesus, Priene, Miletus and Didyma from Selçuk.
  • Days 9 to 10 Aphrodisias and Pamukkale. Fly home via Denizli or İzmir.

Days 1 to 3: Istanbul

Day 1: Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque and the Basilica Cistern, all within a few minutes' walk in Sultanahmet. Day 2: Topkapı Palace and the Istanbul Archaeology Museums next door, among the richest collections of ancient art in the world. Day 3: the Chora (Kariye) mosaics, the Süleymaniye Mosque and a ferry on the Bosphorus.

Istanbul was Byzantium and Constantinople, where for a thousand years scholars copied and taught the ancient texts. See our Istanbul page.

Days 4 to 5: Cappadocia

Fly to Kayseri or Nevşehir in about an hour and a half. Day 4: the Göreme Open-Air Museum and sunset over the Rose Valley. Day 5: a sunrise balloon, then an underground city. If you can spare a third day, follow our 3-day Cappadocia itinerary.

Days 6 to 8: Ephesus and the Ionian coast

Fly to İzmir (usually via Istanbul or Ankara), then take the train or a car to Selçuk, about an hour. Day 7: Ephesus early, the Terrace Houses, then the museum and the Temple of Artemis. Day 8: Priene, Miletus and Didyma, the cities of the first philosophers. See things to do in Ephesus.

Days 9 to 10: Aphrodisias and Pamukkale

Day 9: drive inland to Aphrodisias, city of sculptors, then on to Pamukkale for the night. Day 10: walk the travertine terraces at dawn and explore Hierapolis, birthplace of Epictetus, before flying home from Denizli (via Istanbul) or driving back to İzmir.

Getting around

Domestic flights are frequent and usually cheap if booked early. On the Aegean side a rental car gives the most freedom; trains run from İzmir to Selçuk and Denizli, and intercity buses go everywhere. Leave buffer time around balloon flights, which are often cancelled by wind.

Other ways to spend 10 days

Swap Cappadocia for the south: Antalya, Perge and Aspendos, then the Lycian coast. Or go east to Göbekli Tepe, Mount Nemrut and Mardin. All ten of our routes are on the philosophy map.

Frequently asked questions

Is 10 days enough for Turkey?

Ten days is enough for Istanbul, Cappadocia and the Aegean coast if you fly between them. It is not enough to add the east as well.

What is the best order for a Turkey itinerary?

Istanbul first (most international flights land there), then fly to Cappadocia, then fly to İzmir for the Aegean. End in Pamukkale or İzmir.

What is the best time of year?

April to June and September to October: warm, but not too hot for walking around ruins.
