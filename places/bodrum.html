Bodrum

Muğla · 5th–4th c. BC

Herodotus, who wrote history as an "inquiry" (historia), was born here in ancient Halicarnassus. The Mausoleum, one of the Seven Wonders, stood in this city.

Key ideas

  1. History as inquiry

    Herodotus called his book historia, "inquiry": he asked witnesses, wrote what he saw and compared what he was told. Britannica ↗

  2. So it is not forgotten

    His first sentence states his aim: that the deeds of people and great works should not be forgotten with time. Gutenberg ↗

  3. The masters of a Wonder

    The four sides of the Mausoleum were assigned to Scopas, Bryaxis, Leochares and probably Timotheus. Britannica ↗

Listen

Herodotus was born around 484 BC in Halicarnassus, today's Bodrum. He called his book historia, "inquiry", and stated its purpose in the first sentence: that the deeds of people should not be forgotten with time. The city's other fame was the Mausoleum, the tomb built for the Carian king Mausolus by his wife and sister Artemisia, counted among the Seven Wonders. Four of the most famous sculptors of the age made its sculpture. The word "mausoleum" comes from this building.

What we don't know

Herodotus' dates are approximate; Britannica gives even his birthplace with a question mark.

Legacy

Herodotus' method is counted as the beginning of history writing; the word "mausoleum" comes from the tomb of Mausolus.
