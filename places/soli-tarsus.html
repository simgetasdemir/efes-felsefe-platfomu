Soli and Tarsus

Mersin · 3rd–1st c. BC

Chrysippus (c. 280–207 BC), who built the Stoic system, was born in Soli near today's Mersin. Tarsus was the city of Stoics such as Antipater.

Who: Chrysippus, Antipater of Tarsus

Key ideas

  1. A three-part system

    Logic, physics and ethics form one system, which the Stoics likened to a living being or an egg. Ethics is the goal, but virtue needs all three. SEP §1.2 ↗

  2. The cylinder and the cone

    Every event has a cause, but an external push does not settle everything: the cylinder rolls, the cone spins. Chrysippus argued that our assent is therefore “up to us”. SEP §2.8 ↗

  3. Co-fated events

    Calling the doctor and the doctor’s coming are links in one causal chain. This answers the “lazy argument” that says whatever will be, will be. SEP §2.8 ↗

  4. Only bodies exist

    For the Stoics only bodies exist. Incorporeals such as void, place, time and what is said (lekton) “subsist” without full being. SEP §2.1 ↗

Listen

Chrysippus was born in Soli around 280 BC. He went to Athens, became a student of Cleanthes and succeeded him as head of the Stoic school. He is said to have written hundreds of works, but none survives whole. He turned Stoic logic, physics and ethics into a system, which is why he is counted as the second founder of the Stoa. In later centuries Tarsus too produced Stoic teachers such as Antipater.

Timeline

  1. c. 230–206 BC

    Chrysippus headed the Stoa after Cleanthes; SEP calls him the school’s foremost theorist.

  2. —

    Reportedly wrote over 150 works; none survives whole.

  3. 2nd c. BC

    Antipater of Tarsus became head of the school and answered the Academic Carneades at length.

What we don't know

Because the Stoics’ own works are lost, we know the system mostly from hostile witnesses. SEP also finds the details of the cylinder-and-cone analogy difficult.

Legacy

Stoic philosophy of language shaped ancient grammarians, and natural law thinking influenced Christian writers. Whether Frege was influenced by Stoic logic is debated.
