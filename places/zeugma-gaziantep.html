Listen

Zeugma was a city at a crossing of the Euphrates; its name means "bridge" or "joining" in Greek. The floors of its wealthy houses were laid with scenes from myth and theatre. The mosaics rescued in excavations, before dam waters covered part of the city, are now in the Zeugma Mosaic Museum, opened in 2011. The gaze of its emblem, the Gypsy Girl, seems to follow you wherever you stand. This is an art stop: you see the stories philosophy told, in pictures.

What we don't know

The museum is often called "the largest mosaic museum in the world"; the area figures given in sources do not agree.

Legacy

The mosaics of Zeugma open a way to see the stories of the ancient world without reading a book.
