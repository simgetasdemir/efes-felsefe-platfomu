Privacy and cookies

Antikyol has no accounts and no sign-up. This page explains what is stored in your browser, what is shared with other companies, and how to say no. Türkçesi aşağıda.

What stays in your browser

Your passport stamps (fh-muhur), your language choice (fh-dil) and your progress in the Ephesus app are saved in your own browser's local storage. They never leave your device and we cannot see them. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Some place pages and travel guides show ads served by Google. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to show ads, to limit how often you see the same ad and to measure results. Ads may be personalised based on your earlier visits to this and other websites, unless you opt out. The map, the globe, the walks and the passport show no ads.

  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time with the privacy link Google shows at the bottom of those pages.
  • Anyone can turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings, or for many companies at once at aboutads.info.
  • Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Affiliate links

Hotel, tour and book buttons link to Hotels.com, GetYourGuide, Viator and bookshops with our partner code. If you book through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Those sites set their own cookies once you arrive there, under their own privacy policies. Antikyol sets no tracking cookie of its own.

The Kâtip assistant

When you ask the Kâtip a question in the Ephesus app, the text you type is sent to our server and passed to Anthropic's API to write the answer. We do not store it, and we do not send your name or any account details, because there are none. Do not type personal information into it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and the page requested) to run and protect the service. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
