Before you leave

Sit for a moment

Aphrodisias was a Roman city famous for its sculpture workshops. Alexander lived here in the late 2nd and early 3rd century AD and became the most influential commentator on Aristotle. An inscription found here in 2001 names him as one of the heads of the philosophical schools in Athens. In his treatise On Fate he asked how people could be held responsible if everything were fixed in advance, and argued against the Stoic view of fate.

Think

If everything were fixed in advance, would it be fair to blame you for what you do?
