Walk through Assos

Aristotle, Cleanthes · 4th–3rd c. BC · Ayvacık, Çanakkale

After Plato's death (347 BC) Aristotle came to Assos and stayed about three years. Cleanthes, second head of the Stoic school, was also from Assos.

“All human beings by nature desire to know.”Aristotle, Metaphysics I.1, 980a21

Before you leave

Sit for a moment

When Plato died in 347 BC, Aristotle left the Academy in Athens and came to Assos. He stayed about three years, then crossed to the island of Lesbos opposite, where he studied living things. Another son of Assos, Cleanthes, became the second head of the Stoic school. The story goes that he carried water at night to earn his living and listened to philosophy by day. Look out towards Lesbos from the Temple of Athena: this is the view Aristotle saw.

What have you truly wanted to know lately? Where did that curiosity lead you?
