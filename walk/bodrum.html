Before you leave

Sit for a moment

Herodotus was born around 484 BC in Halicarnassus, today's Bodrum. He called his book historia, "inquiry", and stated its purpose in the first sentence: that the deeds of people should not be forgotten with time. The city's other fame was the Mausoleum, the tomb built for the Carian king Mausolus by his wife and sister Artemisia, counted among the Seven Wonders. Four of the most famous sculptors of the age made its sculpture. The word "mausoleum" comes from this building.
