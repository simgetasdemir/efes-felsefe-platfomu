Before you leave

Sit for a moment

Anaxagoras was born in Clazomenae around 500 BC and moved to Athens; he is counted as the first philosopher to bring philosophy to that city. He held that there is a share of everything in everything, and that the order of the cosmos was set in motion by nous, mind. In Athens he was charged with impiety for saying that the sun was not a god but a burning stone, and he had to leave the city.

Think

Is the order of the world the work of a mind, or does it arise by itself?
