Before you leave

Sit for a moment

Jalal al-Din Rumi was born in 1207 in Balkh, in today’s Afghanistan. His family moved west and finally settled in Konya, the Seljuk capital. In 1244 his meeting with the wandering dervish Shams of Tabriz changed his life. His great work, the Masnavi, opens with the lament of a reed cut from its reed bed and made into a flute. Rumi died in Konya in 1273; his disciples organised the Mevlevi order after him.
