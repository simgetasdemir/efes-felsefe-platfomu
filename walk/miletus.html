Before you leave

Sit for a moment

Miletus was once one of the richest harbour cities of the Aegean. In the 6th century BC three thinkers here tried to explain the world not through stories of the gods but through nature itself. According to Aristotle, Thales took water to be the principle of all things. Anaximander called the source of everything the apeiron, the boundless. Anaximenes looked for it in air. Their answers may sound strange today, but their question is still ours: is there one principle beneath all this variety?

Think

Do you think there is one principle beneath everything, or is the world truly made of many?
