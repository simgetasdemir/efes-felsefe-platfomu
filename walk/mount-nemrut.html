Before you leave

Sit for a moment

Commagene was a small kingdom, but it stood between two great worlds: Greece to the west, Iran to the east. King Antiochus I ruled from 69 to 34 BC and built a temple-tomb for himself on the mountain's summit. The giant figures seated on the terraces are gods, and each bears two names: Zeus is merged with Oromasdes, the Iranian Ahuramazda; Heracles with Artagnes. Climb to the east terrace at sunrise. Among the fallen heads you will see two cultures trying to speak a single language.

Think

Does merging two beliefs or ideas enrich them, or does it take something from both?
