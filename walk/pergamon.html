Walk through Pergamon

Galen · 2nd c. AD · Bergama, İzmir

Home to one of the greatest libraries of the ancient world, and birthplace of Galen (129–c. 200), physician and philosopher.

Acropolis and library

The hill where the books were kept.

IdeaDemonstration

The highest knowledge comes from demonstration built on premises taken from the essence of the subject. Galen ranked premises in four grades: scientific, dialectical, rhetorical and sophistical. SEP ↗

Theatre

IdeaExperience and reason together

He rejected medical schools that relied on experience alone. For him, observation gains meaning within a framework built by reason. SEP ↗

Asclepion

The ancient healing centre: care of body and soul.

IdeaA three-part soul

Reason sits in the brain, spirit in the heart, desire in the liver. He tried to show the brain’s role through a spinal cord experiment on a pig. SEP ↗

Before you leave

Sit for a moment

The library founded by the kings of Pergamon rivalled the one in Alexandria. Galen was born in this city in 129 AD. As a young man he studied with teachers from different philosophical schools, then became a physician and treated gladiators. Later he became physician to emperors in Rome. For Galen a good doctor had to know logic, study nature and live ethically; in other words, he also had to be a philosopher.

Think

Can the health of the body be separated from the health of the soul?
