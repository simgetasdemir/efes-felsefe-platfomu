Before you leave

Sit for a moment

Diogenes was born in Sinope around 404 BC. He fled or was exiled from the city and went to Athens. There, the stories say, he lived in a large jar and walked about in daylight with a lamp, saying he was looking for a human being. He mocked the habits of society in order to question them, and argued that only a life in accordance with nature is free. Most of these stories are anecdotes collected centuries later by Diogenes Laertius.
